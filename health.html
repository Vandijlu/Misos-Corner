Health & Behavior

Signs of Illness

  • Loss of appetite
  • Vomiting or diarrhea
  • Lethargy
  • Sneezing or coughing
  • Changes in litter box habits
  • Excessive hiding

If you notice these signs, contact your veterinarian.

Common Behavioral Issues

  • Jumpiness: Loud noises can scare Miso easily.
  • Hiding: He may hide when afraid or stressed.
  • Curiosity: Curious cats need safe spaces to explore.
  • Play biting: Cats can get excited while playing.

Enrichment Ideas

  • Interactive play with wand toys or mouse toys
  • Cat trees and window perches
  • Puzzle feeders and treat toys
  • Safe hiding spots and cozy beds
  • Sunny places for supervised sunbathing
A happy cat is a healthy cat.
