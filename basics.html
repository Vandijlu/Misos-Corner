Cat Care Basics

The essentials for a happy, healthy cat.

Essential Supplies Checklist

  • Food and water bowls
  • High-quality cat food
  • Litter box and litter
  • Scratching post
  • Toys, especially mouse toys
  • Cat bed or cozy blanket
  • Brush or comb

Litter Box Setup

  • Place in a quiet, low-traffic area.
  • Scoop daily and change litter regularly.
  • Use one litter box per cat, plus one extra.
  • Keep away from food and water bowls.

Vaccination Overview

Vaccinations help protect cats from serious diseases.

  • FVRCP
  • Rabies
  • FeLV, if recommended by a veterinarian
External resource: Cornell Feline Health Center
