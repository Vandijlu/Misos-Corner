Wet Food vs Dry Food

FeatureWet FoodDry Food
MoistureHighLow
StorageRefrigeratedShelf stable

Feeding Schedule

Miso eats twice a day:

  • Morning
  • Evening

When he is ready to be fed, he will come and find me.

Foods Cats Should Avoid

  • Chocolate
  • Onions and garlic
  • Grapes and raisins
  • Alcohol
  • Caffeine
  • Raw dough

Feeding Tips

  • Keep a consistent schedule.
  • Measure portions to avoid overfeeding.
  • Always provide fresh water.
  • Ask a veterinarian about special diet needs.
Always ask your vet about the best diet for your cat.
