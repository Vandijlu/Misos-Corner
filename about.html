About Miso

Hi, I’m Miso!

Miso is a handsome boy with a big personality packed into a sleek black coat.

Personality Traits

  • Scaredy cat, he gets jumpy a lot.
  • Very curious and likes to investigate everything.
  • Sweet and gentle once he feels safe.
  • He comes and finds me when he wants to be fed.

Daily Routine

  • Morning: Eat breakfast
  • Daytime: Explore, nap, and sunbathe
  • Evening: Eat dinner
  • Night: Relax and spend time with family

Fun Facts

  • Favorite toy: mouse
  • Favorite place to sunbathe: a sun spot
  • He is scaredy and jumpy, but still very curious
  • He will come find me when he wants food
Miso may be jumpy, but he is also very loved.
